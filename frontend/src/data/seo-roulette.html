Roulette Bet Types, Without the Noise

Think of bets in two groups: outside bets for steadier play and inside bets for bigger payouts.

Outside bets (more coverage, lower volatility):

  • Red/Black

  • Odd/Even

  • 1–18 / 19–36

  • Dozens: 1–12, 13–24, 25–36

  • Columns: 12 numbers per column

Inside bets (less coverage, higher payouts):

  • Straight Up: single number

  • Split: two adjacent numbers

  • Street: three numbers in a row

  • Corner: four numbers

  • Six Line: six numbers

European vs French vs American Roulette

Two Clean Ways to Play Roulette

No secret systems — just two structures that keep you disciplined.

Option A: Steady Session (Outside-Bet Focused)

  • Pick one even-money bet (red/black or odd/even).

  • Keep the stake consistent for a set number of spins.

  • Only adjust when you change tables or limits, not because of a short run of losses.

Option B: Controlled Upside (Outside Bet + a Few Numbers)

  • Place your main stake on an even-money bet.

  • Add 1–2 straight numbers with small chips.

  • If the straight numbers miss repeatedly, keep them small. Do not grow them to force a hit.

Neither structure changes the odds — that's the point. They keep sessions deliberate instead of reactive.

Common Roulette Mistakes That Drain Players

  • Doubling after losses without respecting table limits. You hit a wall fast.

  • Chasing hot and cold numbers. The wheel does not remember.

  • Covering too many inside numbers. It feels safer, but the payouts shrink and the variance remains.

  • Ignoring speed. Fast roulette is fun, but it turns small leaks into quick losses.

Roulette FAQ

What is the best roulette to play online?

European or French roulette is usually the best starting point, because single-zero wheels have a lower house edge than American roulette.

Is live roulette better than digital roulette?

Live roulette is better for realism and slower pacing. Digital roulette is better for speed and short sessions. Neither changes the math, but the pace changes how quickly you can win or lose.

What is the safest roulette bet?

Even-money outside bets (red/black, odd/even, high/low) are lower volatility than inside bets because they cover 18 numbers.

What does 35:1 mean in roulette?

It's the payout for a straight-up bet on a single number. A winning bet pays 35 units of profit for every 1 unit staked, and your stake comes back too — so a $10 hit returns $360 in total.

Why is American roulette worse for odds?

The extra 00 pocket increases the house edge compared to single-zero wheels.

Do roulette strategies work?

No strategy changes the house edge. The only edge you control is discipline: stake sizing, table selection, and when you stop.

Can I play roulette on mobile at Shuffle?

Yes. Every roulette format is built for touch play and runs in the browser on any phone or tablet — no app needed.

What is La Partage or En Prison?

They are rules found on French-style tables that reduce losses on even-money bets when the ball lands on zero. If you mostly play red/black or odd/even, those tables are worth choosing when available.
