The SHFL Token sits in your account balance alongside your other assets — but if you want to hold it off-platform, you'll need a non-custodial Ethereum wallet: one where you control the private keys. This guide walks you through the process so your funds arrive safely and nothing is lost in the process. We'll cover choosing a wallet that supports SHFL, withdrawing from Shuffle, and solving the most common issues, from missing tokens to network fees.

What kind of wallet supports SHFL?

Any non-custodial wallet that supports Ethereum ERC-20 tokens can hold SHFL — there's no dedicated SHFL wallet, and none is needed. Non-custodial means you — not a company — hold the private keys and control the funds.

Common options (referenced for illustrative purposes, not as endorsements):

  • Browser and mobile wallets like MetaMask or Trust Wallet — free, quick to set up, and the most common choice for interacting with Ethereum apps.

  • Hardware wallets like Ledger or Trezor — physical devices that keep your keys offline, suited to larger balances held long term.

Whichever you choose, download it only from the official website or app store listing, and store your recovery phrase offline. Anyone with that phrase controls your funds.

Do not withdraw SHFL to a centralized exchange. Deposit addresses at exchanges like Binance or Coinbase are custodial, and if the exchange doesn't list SHFL, it cannot credit tokens sent to it. Funds sent this way are almost always permanently lost. Always withdraw to a wallet you control.

How to add SHFL to your wallet

Wallets don't display every token automatically — you may need to import SHFL before it appears.

In MetaMask:

  1. Open your wallet and select "Import tokens".

  2. Search for SHFL, or paste the official contract address.

  3. Confirm the import.

Your SHFL balance will now display alongside your other assets. The process is similar in most ERC-20 wallets: look for an "add token" or "import token" option and use the contract address.

How to withdraw SHFL from Shuffle

  1. Copy your wallet's Ethereum address — in MetaMask, click the account name to copy it.

  2. From the top of any site page, open "Wallet" and select "Withdraw".

  3. Select "SHFL" as the asset.

  4. Paste your wallet address into the ERC-20 address field.

  5. Enter the amount and confirm with your two-factor authentication. Shuffle doesn't charge a fee for SHFL withdrawals.

No additional account verification is required to withdraw — the basic details provided when you deposit cover it.

Before you confirm, check the address character by character and make sure you're withdrawing on the Ethereum network. Blockchain transactions can't be reversed once sent.

Why do you need ETH in your wallet?

Every transaction on Ethereum — sending tokens, swapping them, or interacting with any app — requires a fee paid in ETH, known as gas. This applies to SHFL like any other ERC-20 token: a wallet holding only SHFL cannot move it.

This is the most common point of confusion for new wallet users. If your wallet shows an "insufficient funds" error despite a full SHFL balance, it means you're missing ETH for the fee, not SHFL. These fees are charged by the network's validators, not by Shuffle, and they fluctuate with congestion. To comfortably cover them, Shuffle recommends sending at least $20 worth of ETH to your wallet address.

Troubleshooting SHFL withdrawals

SHFL isn't showing in my wallet

The most likely cause is that the token hasn't been imported — wallets don't display unrecognized tokens by default. Follow the import steps above. To confirm the withdrawal itself arrived, paste your wallet address into Etherscan and check the token balance there; if SHFL shows on Etherscan but not in your wallet, it's an import issue, not a missing transaction.

My transaction is stuck or pending

Ethereum transactions can slow down when the network is congested (otherwise known as gas). Copy the transaction hash from your withdrawal history and look it up on Etherscan to see its live status. Pending transactions should confirm on their own once congestion eases.

I sent SHFL to the wrong address or an exchange

Blockchain transactions are irreversible, and Shuffle no longer has access to the funds once they have been sent off-site. If you sent SHFL to a centralized exchange, contact that exchange's support — recovery is occasionally possible but never guaranteed. This is why double-checking the address before confirming matters more than any other step in this guide.

Can you swap SHFL for other crypto?

Yes — once SHFL is in your own wallet, decentralized exchanges like Uniswap support swapping it for ETH and other tokens. Note that this is the only route out of SHFL: Shuffle's on-platform Convert feature works in one direction only, into SHFL.

For the full swap process, including fees and how conversion works on-platform, see our SHFL convert and swap guide. Decentralized exchanges are third-party platforms independent of Shuffle — always verify you're on the official site and do your own research before trading.

FAQ

Do I need a special wallet for SHFL?

No — there's no dedicated SHFL wallet. Any non-custodial wallet that supports Ethereum ERC-20 tokens works, including MetaMask, Trust Wallet, and hardware wallets like Ledger or Trezor.

Can I withdraw SHFL to Binance or Coinbase?

No. Centralized exchanges that don't list SHFL cannot credit it, and tokens sent to their deposit addresses are almost always unrecoverable. Withdraw only to a non-custodial wallet you control.

How long do SHFL withdrawals take?

Withdrawals are processed on the Ethereum network, and confirmation times depend on network congestion. Most complete within minutes; you can track any transaction live on Etherscan using its transaction hash.

Is there a fee to withdraw SHFL?

No. Shuffle doesn't charge a fee for SHFL withdrawals. Once your tokens are in your own wallet, sending or swapping them requires Ethereum network fees paid in ETH, charged by the network's validators rather than by Shuffle. A balance of at least $20 worth of ETH is recommended to cover them.

Can I convert SHFL back to Bitcoin on Shuffle?

No. At this time, SHFL Convert is only in one direction. In order to exchange SHFL for another crypto, you must withdraw it to an external wallet and swap on a decentralized exchange.
