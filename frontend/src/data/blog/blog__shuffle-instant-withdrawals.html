What counts as "instant"?

There is no industry wide legal definition of an instant withdrawal, so this article uses a measurable one: a withdrawal is instant if it is approved and settled within one minute of the request.

That threshold matters because a crypto withdrawal has two stages. First, the operator processes your request and broadcasts the transaction to the blockchain. Second, the network confirms it into a block and the funds land in your wallet. Shuffle controls the first stage. The blockchain controls the second. When a review site says a withdrawal "took 20 minutes," it is almost always measuring both stages together on a slower chain — not a delay on Shuffle's side.

Keeping those two stages separate is the only way to compare sources fairly, and it is how the findings below are presented. It is also what makes withdrawal speed uniquely checkable at a crypto casino: every payout produces a public transaction hash, so you can look up any withdrawal of your own on a block explorer and verify the timing for yourself.

Why is your Shuffle withdrawal in review?

If your withdrawal shows as under review, it has been flagged for KYC — a check of your identity documents — and it will not move until verification is complete. This is the one scenario in which a Shuffle withdrawal is not instant, and it is the most common cause of payout complaints, so it deserves a straight answer rather than a footnote.

Reviewers are aligned on both the problem and the fix. CryptoSlate's review identifies KYC checks — typically triggered after a sizeable or bonus-linked win — as the most common complaint against the platform, and notes that a flagged withdrawal can wait days while documents are checked. Tokenist reviewed a widely shared Reddit thread alleging Shuffle withheld funds and concluded the complaints described standard verification holds, not lost money or manipulated games — the same checks every licensed operator runs.

The fix is the same in every source: verify early. Complete identity verification when you sign up, before you have a win waiting, and respond promptly if additional documents are requested. A verified account is what keeps the under-a-minute experience intact.

There is no fixed dollar threshold that triggers a check. Verification is risk-based and can be requested at any time, because Shuffle operates under a Curaçao Gaming Control Board license (Natural Nine B.V., license OGL/2024/1337/0628) that carries identity and anti-money-laundering obligations — our guide to Shuffle's Curaçao license, explained covers exactly what the license requires and why. The same license is why those checks cannot be skipped — by Shuffle or by you. It is also why verifying early works: the check has to happen eventually, and an account that has already cleared it has nothing left to trigger.
