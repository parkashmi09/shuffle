Slot Features Worth Knowing

Modern slots go far beyond spinning reels. The features you will meet most often across the Shuffle library:

  • Wilds and scatters — wilds substitute for standard symbols to complete wins; scatters trigger free spins and bonus rounds without needing to line up.

  • Free spins and multipliers — bonus rounds played without staking your balance, often boosted by sticky wilds, expanding symbols, or growing multipliers.

  • Cascading reels and cluster pays — winning symbols clear and new ones drop in, allowing chains of consecutive wins from a single spin.

  • Megaways — dynamic reel engines that change the number of symbols per spin, creating up to hundreds of thousands of ways to win.

  • Bonus buy — direct purchase of a bonus round at a fixed cost, available where local regulations permit.

  • Jackpots — fixed or progressive prizes that trigger randomly or through bonus mechanics on selected titles.
