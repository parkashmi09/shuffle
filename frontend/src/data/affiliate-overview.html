Commission Structure

Sports

Every sports bet generates commission at 3% of the wagered amount. The commission is calculated using this formula:

(0.03 * Wagered Amount * Commission Rate) / 2

Casino

Every game on Shuffle generates commission based on it's house edge. The commission is calculated using this formula:

(Edge * Wagered Amount * Commission Rate) / 2
