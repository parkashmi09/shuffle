How Baccarat Works

Baccarat looks complicated when people mention drawing charts, but the loop itself is simple: you bet on the outcome, the table follows fixed dealing rules, and the winning hand is the one closest to 9.

Card values: Aces count as 1, cards 2–9 count as face value, and 10/J/Q/K count as 0. Only the last digit matters, so a 15 becomes a 5.
