How To Play

Playing the SHFL Lottery

1. Choose your entry type

Choose between Standard and Powerplay tickets. Standard entries are the cheapest way to play and Powerplay increases your chance to win!

2. Select number of entries

Pick how many entries you want for the upcoming lottery. You can enter with as many tickets as you want.

3. Choose your numbers

Fast fill or choose your own. You can fast fill an individual ticket or fast fill every ticket with randomly generated numbers.

4. Pay & confirm your entry

By clicking confirm, you now have a chance to win 9 tiers of prizes in the upcoming SHFL Lottery.

Checking the results

Checking your numbers

After the draw concludes, you can view your winning numbers by going to the Winning Tickets tab.

Claiming your winnings

You can claim your winnings by clicking claim on an individual winning ticket. The winnings are instantly credited to your balance.

Other ways to play

By staking SHFL, you can receive free perpetual entries into the SHFL Lottery. You can stake SHFL on the SHFL Dashboard.

Watch the draw LIVE

The SHFL Lottery Stream begins 1 hour before the draw closes at 6:00am UTC and can be viewed on our Kick channel.

Entry types

Standard Entry is your standard single ticket. It is eligible for only the upcoming draw.

By selecting Powerplay, the Powerball number is guaranteed increasing your chance at winning the jackpot by 18x.

By staking SHFL you get free perpetual entries into the SHFL lottery. You can stake your SHFL for free by staking on the Token Dashboard.
