What is a recent play bonus?

Recent play bonuses give you money back based on your gameplay during your latest rank. If you had a rough streak of luck, your recent play will be increased. If you made a profit while ranking up, you will still receive a base recent play bonus. 

What is a reload bonus?

Reloads give you a fixed amount of bonus funds depending on the rank you reach, which are available to claim at each day for a set period of time!
