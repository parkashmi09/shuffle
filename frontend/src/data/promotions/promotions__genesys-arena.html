How to Win in the Genesys Arena Tournament

  • To qualify for the leaderboard, players must purchase at least one in-game Bonus/Free Spins feature ("Bonus Buy") in any participating game.

  • Bonus/Free Spins features triggered through normal gameplay do not contribute to the tournament score.

  • Ante bets (including, but not limited to, Super Spin, Double Chance Bet, and Triple Chance Bet) do not contribute to the tournament score.

  • For scoring purposes, win multipliers are calculated using the game’s Base Bet (not the Bonus Buy purchase price). Formula: Win Multiplier = Total Win ÷ Base Bet.

  • The Base Bet is the standard bet level set within the game before the Bonus Buy is purchased. Example: a €100 Bonus Buy in Sweet Rush Bonanza corresponds to a €1 Base Bet.

  • Each Bonus Buy is treated as a single qualifying game round for scoring purposes, regardless of the number of spins included within the feature.

  • A player's tournament score is the sum of all rounded win multipliers from qualifying Bonus Buys during the tournament.

  • Win multipliers are rounded to the nearest whole number before being added to the score.

  • Multipliers greater than 0.5 are rounded up, while multipliers of 0.5 or less are rounded down.

Score Calculation Example (Illustrative Only)

Formula: Score = Win(1) / Bet(1) + ... + Win(n) / Bet(n)

  • Bonus Buy: €100 (Base Bet €1), Win: €500 → Score = €500 ÷ €1 = 500

  • Regular Bet: €5, Win: €1,000 →Not counted (only qualifying Bonus Buys contribute)

  • Bonus Buy: €200 (Base Bet €2), Win: €1,000 → Score = €1,000 ÷ €2 =500

Final Score = 500 + 500 = 1,000
